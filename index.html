ABOUT THE SOFTWARE

What BioPharm-Pack actually does

Input: drug/product, dosage form, strength, ingredients and storage target.

Process: risk screening → packaging requirements → biodegradable material/architecture concept → 3D visualization → validation plan.

Output: a research decision-support record, not a regulatory approval or a substitute for pharmacopoeial testing.
