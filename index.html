COST LOGIC

Why finished cost can differ

  • Material grade and pharmaceutical-contact suitability can change cost.
  • Barrier coatings or multilayer structures may be needed.
  • Conversion, forming, sealing, printing and tooling add cost.
  • Small research quantities can have higher effective unit cost.
  • Supplier, MOQ, transport and quality testing can materially change the final figure.

LEGAL & COMPLIANCE CENTER

Research-use legal documentation

These documents are protective project language, not a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer or regulatory professional.

1. Research Prototype & Regulatory Disclaimer

BioPharm-Pack is a research, educational and innovation prototype intended to support preliminary pharmaceutical packaging research. The platform does not constitute regulatory approval, certification, legal advice, quality assurance, GMP certification, pharmacopoeial certification, sterility assurance, contamination-free certification, or a determination that any packaging system is suitable for manufacture, marketing or human use.

Any screening result is a decision-support output based on the information entered and the evidence available to the platform. A positive screening result must not be interpreted as proof of pharmaceutical suitability. Material characterization, packaging performance, product–package compatibility, stability, container-closure integrity, extractables/leachables and other applicable studies must be performed by appropriately qualified personnel before any real pharmaceutical application.

2. India Regulatory Notice

In India, drugs are regulated under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945, with responsibilities shared between central and state regulators. Applicable packaging, labelling, manufacturing, licensing, quality and distribution requirements depend on the product and use case. The platform does not replace those requirements.

The CDSCO publishes the Drugs Rules and regulatory notifications on its official website. Users must check the current official requirements applicable to their product.

3. Packaging Claims

The terms “biodegradable”, “compostable”, “bio-based” and similar environmental claims are not interchangeable. A claim should be supported by appropriate material, test and end-of-life evidence. The platform must not be used to create an unsupported environmental claim.

4. Data & Privacy Notice

This prototype is designed to operate as a local research interface. Do not enter confidential, proprietary, patient-identifiable, unpublished clinical, trade-secret or other sensitive information unless the deployment has an appropriate security and governance system. Local browser storage is not identity verification and should not be treated as secure enterprise storage.

5. Intellectual Property & Sources

Official standards, pharmacopoeial monographs and regulatory documents may be protected by copyright or access restrictions. BioPharm-Pack should link to authorized sources rather than reproduce protected content without permission. Source version and access date should be retained for traceability.

6. User Responsibility

The user is responsible for verifying source currency, product-specific requirements, applicable Indian and international regulations, experimental results, and conclusions before using any output for research, manufacturing, regulatory submission or commercial decisions.

7. No Warranty

The prototype is provided for research and demonstration purposes. To the extent permitted by applicable law, no warranty is made regarding completeness, accuracy, uninterrupted operation or fitness for a particular purpose.

SOURCE CONTROL

Core packaging principles in this prototype are aligned to WHO Guidelines on Packaging for Pharmaceutical Products (TRS 902 Annex 9), FDA's final 1999 container-closure guidance, and ISO 15378:2017 with its 2024 amendment. ASTM D6400-26 is used only when discussing the specific claim of aerobic municipal/industrial compostability of applicable plastics/products.
